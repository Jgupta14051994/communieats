## App Store Description (4000 chars max)

CommuniEats is the food delivery app that rewards you for being a good neighbor.

**Order food from 12+ local restaurants and choose how you want it:**

🚗 **Delivery** — Standard delivery to your door
🚶 **Pick Up** — Go yourself and save 10%
🌱 **Community Courier** — Pick up your order AND deliver a neighbor's on your way home. Earn 20-30% off YOUR meal while helping your community.

**How Community Courier works:**
1. Add items to your cart from any restaurant
2. Choose "Community Courier" at checkout
3. See neighbors who have orders ready at the same restaurant
4. Accept 1-2 neighbor deliveries
5. Get up to 30% off your own order
6. Drop off neighbor orders on your way home — they get their food faster, you save money, and everyone reduces delivery emissions

**Discover what's hot near you:**
- Live order count badges show you which restaurants are popular today
- Community map shows real-time order activity with heat bubbles
- "Trending Near You" shows the busiest spots right now

**Your Sustainability Dashboard tracks:**
- Total CO₂ saved by choosing Community Courier
- Deliveries completed for your community
- Lifetime discounts earned
- Your Community Hero rank on the leaderboard

**Tier System:**
- New Member → Rising Star → Community Hero → Legend
- Earn points for every delivery, climb the leaderboard

Join thousands of neighbors making food delivery better for everyone.

Download CommuniEats today — eat well, save money, help your community.
