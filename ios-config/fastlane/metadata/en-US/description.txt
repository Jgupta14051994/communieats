CommuniEats is the food delivery app that rewards you for being a good neighbor.

Order food from local restaurants and choose how you want it:

🚗 DELIVERY — Standard delivery to your door
🚶 PICK UP — Go yourself and save 10%
🌱 COMMUNITY COURIER — Pick up your order AND deliver a neighbor's order on your way home. Earn 20-30% off YOUR meal while helping your community.

HOW COMMUNITY COURIER WORKS:
1. Add items to your cart from any restaurant
2. Choose "Community Courier" at checkout
3. See neighbors who have orders ready at the same restaurant
4. Accept 1 or 2 neighbor deliveries
5. Get up to 30% off your own order instantly
6. Drop off neighbor orders on your way home — they get their food faster, you save money, and everyone reduces delivery vehicle trips
